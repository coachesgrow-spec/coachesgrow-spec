# Martin Yovchevski | Мартин Йовчевски

I’m the co-founder and technical lead of [Unplag.bg](https://www.unplag.bg/), a Bulgarian platform that helps educators review text similarity and sources in academic work. I am also CEO at [RikoStyle](https://rikostyle.com/), a Bulgarian manufacturer of chairs and tables.

My interests include Bulgarian-language technology, education and the responsible use of AI. I believe software should make evidence easier to understand while leaving important decisions to people.
